PinTally — please read

PinTally keeps your golf scores, drive distances, yards-to-the-pin, and any swing videos you record on this device only. No account, no sign-in, nothing is sent anywhere. GPS distances come from your phone and are a guide — typically within about 5–10 yards under open sky — not a laser rangefinder.

By using PinTally you agree to the Terms in the Help tab: the app is provided as-is by White Wolf Apps, scores and distances are for your own recreational use, and you are responsible for watching where you walk and swing.

Help

Your golfer profile: The first time you open PinTally, you create your golfer profile — your name is the one thing it needs; a photo and your Instagram/Facebook are optional. That profile is Player 1: your name shows on your scoring rows, your scorecard, and your past games. Change it any time in My profile above and Player 1 updates with it. Your profile lives on this phone only — PinTally has no accounts yet, and nothing about it is uploaded anywhere.

Share my round: Open any past game and tap “📤 Share my round” to send a plain-text recap — your name, the course, your score, your best hole and longest drive — through your phone's usual share options, or copy it to paste anywhere. The app itself never posts anything.

Map a course yourself: If a course isn't in the app, start it under “Map a course yourself” on the Play tab, then as you play each hole tap “📍 I'm at the tee” on the tee box and “📍 I'm at the hole” at the flag. PinTally figures the hole's yardage from those two spots, may suggest a par (tap to accept — it never changes a par on its own), and remembers the whole map on this phone for every future round. The “Mapped: n of N holes” count shows your progress, and a mapped hole works like any other: saved tee for one-tap shot measuring, saved pin for yards to the pin.

Go Golf: Tap Go Golf, pick where you're playing — everything else fills itself in. At the start of a round you can choose 9 or 18 holes: on a 9-hole course, 18 plays the same 9 twice and holes 10–18 share holes 1–9's pars, yardages, tees and pins; on an 18-hole course, 9 plays the front 9 only. If a round is already going, Go Golf offers to resume it or start a new one.

Keep score: The score boxes are right at the top of the Play tab — open the app and you're looking at this hole's scores. Use the big − / + buttons for each player, the quick number row under them, or tap the 🎤 mic and just say the number (one tap, then speak). Totals vs par update on the Card tab.

Saving: Every score, drive, and pin saves to this device the moment you enter it — nothing is lost if you close the app. When a hole is done, tap the big “✓ Save Hole” button: it turns into “Hole saved ✓”, the hole number fills in green in the saved row, the hole gets a ✓ on the scorecard, and the app moves you to the next hole. Tap any hole number in the saved row to jump straight to that hole. Walking to the next hole with the Next button saves the one you just left, too. If you change anything on a saved hole, the button flips back to “Save Hole” so you know to tap it again.

Measure my shot (the easy way): Stand where you hit from and tap “📍 I'm where I hit from”. Walk to your ball — the yards count up live — then tap “📍 I'm at my ball”. The distance shows big and saves as that hole's drive on the card. The spot you hit from also saves as that hole's tee for this course: next round here, the tee is already saved, so you just walk to your ball and tap the one button. Tee in the wrong spot? “Move tee to where I'm standing” on the Measure card replaces it.

Drive / shot distance (the two-button way): On the tee, tap “Mark tee”. Walk to your ball and tap “Mark ball”. PinTally shows the yards between the two spots and saves it as that hole's drive on the scorecard. Marking a tee this way saves it as the course's tee for the hole, too — and if a tee is already saved, “Mark ball” measures from it.

Past games: The Past games tab lists every finished round — date, course, each player's total vs par, and holes played. Tap “See game” for the full hole-by-hole card, your best hole, and your longest drive, plus any swing videos from that round (tap one to watch its replay with tracer and drive distance). A round you're still playing shows at the top of Past games with a Resume button.

Find a course: At the bottom of the Play tab, “Find courses near me” uses your location, or search by course name or town — both need internet; everything else in PinTally works offline. Scorecard details for most courses aren't published in one place, so a found course starts with par 4 on every hole and blank yardages — edit them under “Hole setup & pin” on the Play tab and your edits save to that course. Found courses stay in the course list on this device. If a name search comes up empty, the app offers to search the courses near that town instead — and if a course just isn't on the map anywhere, “Map it yourself” lets you map it as you play it.

Hole map: The hole map is a simple diagram drawn from the spots you've marked — tee, your ball, and the pin — with the real distances between them. It isn't a picture of the actual fairway; courses don't publish that.

Yards to the pin: The first time you reach a green, stand by the flag and tap “Set pin here”. From then on, that hole shows your live yards to the pin. Pins are saved on your device per course, so they are still there next round. GPS needs location permission and open sky; accuracy is usually about 5–10 yards.

Swing video & tracer: On the Play tab, tap “🎥 Film my swing”, prop the phone where it can see you and the ball (behind you or off to the side, steady, a few steps back), take your swing, and tap Stop — the replay plays right away, with slow-motion and scrub controls for a closer look. One video per hole; filming a hole again replaces its old video.

To show the ball flight, use the tracer on the replay: pause where the swing starts, pick “✏️ Tap the flight path” and tap a few points along where the ball flew, or pick “🏹 Quick arc” and tap where the ball took off and where it landed (you can drag the top of the arc up or down). The glowing line then plays back in time with the video. Being straight with you: the tracer is drawn by your taps — a phone video of a small, fast ball isn't something any app can reliably spot on its own, so PinTally doesn't pretend to find the ball automatically. Your taps are what make the line honest.

Distance on the replay: If you marked the tee and ball for that hole, the replay shows “Drive: NNN yd” from those marks. Mark the ball when you walk up to it and the stamp updates the next time you open the replay.

Quail Ridge (Grove, OK): Built in from its published scorecard — 9 holes, par 36, Blue tees 3,023 yards. Playing 18? Start it with Go Golf and choose 18 holes — the same 9 repeat as holes 10–18, sharing the same tees and pins. (The separate “Quail Ridge — 18” course entry is still there too.) No published green GPS exists for Quail Ridge, so set each pin once with “Set pin here”.

Any other course: Choose “Any course”, set 9 or 18 holes, and edit par and yards right on the Play tab — your edits save automatically.

Your data: Everything — scores, rounds, pins, and swing videos — lives only on this device in your browser storage. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Videos take real space on your phone, so delete ones you don't want from the replay screen. Deleting a saved round also deletes that round's swing videos, and starting a new round clears the current card's videos too. Clearing browser data erases everything, so keep this device/browser if your rounds matter to you.
