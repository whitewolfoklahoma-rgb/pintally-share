PinTally — please read

PinTally keeps your golf scores, drive distances, and yards-to-the-pin on this device only. No account, no sign-in, nothing is sent anywhere. GPS distances come from your phone and are a guide — typically within about 5–10 yards under open sky — not a laser rangefinder.

Help

Keep score: Pick your course, then on each hole use the big − / + buttons for each player, or tap the 🎤 mic and just say the number (one tap, then speak). Totals vs par update on the Card tab.

Saving: Every score, drive, and pin saves to this device the moment you enter it — nothing is lost if you close the app. When a hole is done, tap the big “✓ Save Hole” button: it turns into “Hole saved ✓”, the hole number fills in green in the saved row, and the hole gets a ✓ on the scorecard, so you can see at a glance which holes are recorded. Walking to the next hole saves the one you just left, too. If you change anything on a saved hole, the button flips back to “Save Hole” so you know to tap it again.

Drive / shot distance: On the tee, tap “Mark tee”. Walk to your ball and tap “Mark ball”. PinTally shows the yards between the two spots and saves it as that hole's drive on the scorecard.

Yards to the pin: The first time you reach a green, stand by the flag and tap “Set pin here”. From then on, that hole shows your live yards to the pin. Pins are saved on your device per course, so they are still there next round. GPS needs location permission and open sky; accuracy is usually about 5–10 yards.

Quail Ridge (Grove, OK): Built in from its published scorecard — 9 holes, par 36, Blue tees 3,023 yards. Playing 18? Choose “Quail Ridge — 18 (play the 9 twice)” and the same 9 repeat as holes 10–18. No published green GPS exists for Quail Ridge, so set each pin once with “Set pin here”.

Any other course: Choose “Any course”, set 9 or 18 holes, and edit par and yards right on the Play tab — your edits save automatically.

Your data: Everything — scores, rounds, pins — lives only on this device in your browser storage. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. To back up, finish rounds and keep this device/browser; clearing browser data erases your rounds.
